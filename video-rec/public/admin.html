How to Use AirPlay

  1. Make sure your iPad and Apple TV (or AirPlay receiver) are on the same Wi-Fi network
  2. Open Control Center on your iPad (swipe down from top-right corner)
  3. Tap the Screen Mirroring button
  4. Select your Apple TV or AirPlay device
  5. Enter the AirPlay code if prompted
  6. Your iPad screen will now mirror to your TV
  7. Use "Share Screen" button in this app to share with users
